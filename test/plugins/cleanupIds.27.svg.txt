Preserves IDs when a link to a JavaScript URI is present, regardless of the
capitalization of the scheme.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" xmlns:xlink="http://www.w3.org/1999/xlink">
    <a xlink:href="JavaScript:alert('uwu')">uwu</a>
    <circle id="circle001" fill="red" cx="60" cy="60" r="50"/>
</svg>
