Removes unreferenced IDs when an element named script belongs to another
namespace, which is not a script and does not deoptimize the plugin.

===

<svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" xmlns:foo="http://example.com/foo">
    <foo:script>
        private data
    </foo:script>
    <circle id="circle001" fill="red" cx="60" cy="60" r="50"/>
</svg>
